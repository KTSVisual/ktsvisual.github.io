BEHIND THE VISUAL.

I'm Kian Taylor-Sanderson, a third-year ALT LVDP student and the person behind KTS Visual. My work spans collaborative studio productions, creative experimentation, photography and filmmaking.

I've contributed to university installations and team projects, worked on screen mapping, documented live music through photography and explored the production process through my first short film, Mistakes in Crimson. I enjoy combining technical problem-solving with visual ideas and working alongside other creatives to bring projects to life.
